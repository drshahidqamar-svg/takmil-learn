📘 Key Concept
Multiplication = Repeated Addition
7 × 8 means adding 8 seven times:
8 + 8 + 8 + 8 + 8 + 8 + 8 = 56
Step-by-step · مرحلہ بمرحلہ
1
Start: 8 (first group)
2
Add 8 again → 16 (two groups)
…
Continue 7 times → 56 ✓
💡 Memory Trick · یاد رکھیں
Think: "7 ate (8) = 56" — "ate" sounds like "8", so 7 × 8 is always 56!
